How this works

Filter deals each card once: Know it means you could explain it to someone; Fuzzy means you've heard of it but couldn't; New to me means it's new. Quiz then tests the ones you claimed, and a wrong answer honestly downgrades the claim.

Answers stay in this browser and sync — no account, no login — to a private ledger the reading tools consult, so recommendations stop guessing what you already know. A few cards arrive pre-marked from reading history; override them freely.
